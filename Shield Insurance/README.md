**📌  Project Overview:**

As part of the Codebasics Virtual Internship – Week 4, I analyzed the Shield Insurance dataset and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard.   
The dashboard provides an overview of the business’s performance, focusing on revenue and customer metrics. 
It also explores trends across customer demographics, sales modes,revenue analysis and age groups to highlight patterns in business growth and performance.



**📑 Dashboard Pages:**

🏠 Home – Navigation and project overview
	
📊 Overview – Revenue, Customers, key KPIs, and overall business performance

📈 Sales Mode Analysis – Performance analysis across different sales modes
	
👥 Age Group Analysis – Revenue, customers, premiums, and policy preferences across age groups 






**📊 KPIs Analyzed:** 

• Total Revenue

• Total Customers

• Revenue Growth Rate

• Customer Growth Rate

• Month-over-Month Revenue Change

• Month-over-Month Customer Change

• Daily Revenue Growth

• Daily Customer Growth






**🔍 Key Business Questions Addressed:**

• How is revenue and customer performance changing over time?

• Which sales modes contribute to business performance?

• How does performance vary across different age groups?

• What are the monthly and daily growth trends?

• Where can the business identify areas for further analysis and improvement?





**💡🔍 Key Business Insights:(a short summary):** 

• Revenue peaked in March and declined in April.

• Offline-Agent was the leading sales mode in terms of revenue and customers.

• Premium and policy preferences varied across different age groups.
