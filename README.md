**📌  Project Overview:**

As part of the Codebasics Virtual Internship, I analyzed a Power BI dashboard provided for the WaveCon Telecom 5G business case. The objective was to evaluate the impact of the 5G rollout on revenue, customer behavior, recharge plans, and market performance. Based on the dashboard insights, I answered key stakeholder questions and prepared a presentation with actionable business recommendations.

**🎯 Business Problem:** 
WaveCon Telecom recently launched 5G services across major cities in India. The objective of this project was to evaluate the impact of the 5G rollout on revenue, customer behavior, recharge plans, and market performance. The analysis also identifies opportunities to improve business growth and customer retention.

**📊 KPIs Analyzed:** 
-	Revenue
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
-	TAU (Total Active Users)
-	TUsU (Total Unsubscribed Users)
-	Market Share
-	City Performance
-	Plan Performance

**❓ Business Questions Addressed:**
-	What was the impact of the 5G launch on revenue?
-	How did business performance change before and after the 5G rollout?
-	Which KPIs underperformed after the 5G launch?
-	Which recharge plans performed well, and which underperformed?
-	Were any plans significantly affected by the 5G launch?
-	Were any plans discontinued after the 5G rollout, and why?
-	What opportunities exist to improve customer retention, revenue, and market share?

**💡 Key Insights (a short summary):** 
**Overall Findings**
- Revenue remained almost stable after the 5G launch, declining slightly by 0.5%.
- Higher ARPU (+11%) indicates retained customers spent more.
- Active Users (-8.3%) declined, while Unsubscribed Users (+23.5%) increased, making customer retention the biggest challenge.
- Market share declined from 20.24% to 18.88%, showing that the 5G rollout did not improve WaveCon's competitive position.
- Customers shifted toward newer 5G-ready plans (P1, P11, P12), while older plans (P4–P7) lost demand.

**Strategic Recommendations**
- Increase customer migration through attractive 5G upgrade campaigns.
- Strengthen customer retention and loyalty programs.
- Optimize pricing and benefits of underperforming plans.
- Continuously monitor customer feedback and competitor strategies.
- Focus marketing efforts on cities showing declining KPIs.
